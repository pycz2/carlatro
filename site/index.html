CARLATRO

Cars instead of cards.

A Balatro-like roguelike deckbuilder. Build poker hands from a garage of 52 cars, stack mods, beat the boss races and win the Grand Prix.

▶ Play free in your browser Source on GitHub

Start your engine

The full game runs right here. Your run, settings and statistics are saved in your browser.

Best with a mouse and keyboard. Enter plays a hand, Esc opens the menu. Open in a new tab

Everything in the garage

All the depth of a deckbuilder, drawn and composed entirely in code.

Poker hands with cars

Pair up a Tandem, line up a Gear Shift, park a Same Garage. Score = Chips × Mult.

30 mods

Turbos, drift kings, odometers and more. Each one changes how you score.

Editions

Foil, Holographic, Polychrome and Negative mods, just like the original game.

Sponsors

Permanent bonuses for the whole run: more slots, more hands, cheaper shopping.

Booster packs

Open Garage, Dyno, Workshop and Mod packs and take the best card.

Boss races

8 antes, 12 nasty boss rules, the Grand Prix, then endless mode.

Two looks, one game

Switch the card art and the suits in the settings. Every name and picture follows your choice.

Cars

Muscle, JDM, Euro and Offroad cars. Mods are nitro, turbos and gear shifters.

Classic

How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down.

Pick up to 5 cars

Make the best poker hand you can from the 8 cars in your hand.

Score big

Every scored car adds chips. Mods add Mult.

Chips×Mult

Beat the race

Reach the target score before you run out of hands. Watch out for the boss rule.

Upgrade your ride

Spend your winnings at the Pit Stop and build an unstoppable combo.
